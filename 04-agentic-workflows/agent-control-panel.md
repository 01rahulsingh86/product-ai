## What Juno Is Doing

Juno's risk flagging pillar monitors Jira and Slack for delivery risks (slipping dependencies, blocked tickets, scope creep) and surfaces each one to the PM with linked evidence before it reaches leadership.

## Four Levers

**Stop Conditions** (Loop):

Flag delivery risks (slipping dependencies, blocked tickets, scope creep) from Jira and Slack before they reach leadership, and surface them to the PM with evidence.

Lever 1: Stop Conditions

max_steps: 10 per signal investigated.
Abort if the same tool fails 2x in a row, or if 3 consecutive steps add no new evidence.
Hard timeout: 60s wall clock per signal.

**Structured Tool Outputs** (Tools):

Every tool returns {status: ok | empty | error, count: int, items: [], source_ids: []}.

Jira lookup
Success: status: ok, count >= 1, each item has ticket ID, status, assignee, and last_updated.
Failure: status: error, or count: 0 on a known ticket ID (treated as failed, not "no risk").
Stale check: if last_updated is more than 7 days old, add stale: true.
Slack search
Success: status: ok with a message count and permalinks.
Failure: status: error. count: 0 is valid "empty" only if the channel read itself succeeded.
Dependency lookup
Success: a list of linked tickets with their status.
Failure: status: error or a missing link. Juno must not assume "no dependencies."
Draft/post tool
Success: status: ok plus a draft ID.
Failure: status: error, and Juno never retries a send.

**Confidence Thresholds** (Verification):

High (0.85+ and at least 2 independent sources): Juno posts the flag to the PM's Juno inbox on its own, with evidence links.
Medium (0.60 to 0.84, or only 1 source): Goes into the PM's daily digest labeled "needs your check." Not pushed as an alert.
Low (below 0.60): Juno does not surface it. It logs the signal for the weekly review and takes no action.

**North Star** (Context):

Surface real delivery risks to the PM early, each backed by a linked source, so the PM decides what to do. Juno never messages stakeholders, edits tickets, or states a risk without a cited ticket or message.

Agency Permission

Can decide alone: read signals, investigate, score confidence, and flag to the PM only.
Must propose and wait: re-prioritizing work, messaging stakeholders or engineers, changing ticket status or timelines, and escalating to leadership. Juno can draft these, but the PM sends them.

Access Control

Runs under the PM's own permissions, not company-wide access.
Read-only on Jira, Slack, and the dependency tracker.
Out of reach: HR and performance data, private DMs, finance and legal channels, and any channel the PM isn't in.

Fallback Protocols

If a stop condition fires, Juno stops immediately and hands the case back to the PM with what it found, the step where it stopped, and the reason.
It never makes a 3rd retry on a failed tool, and never guesses to fill a gap.
If the tool error is on Juno's side, the flag is marked "unverified."

Checkpoints
A person must review before Juno continues when:

Any step would message someone other than the PM.
A flag involves a P0 or an executive-visible project.
Juno wants to escalate or change any ticket or timeline.
Evidence conflicts between sources (for example, Jira says "done" but Slack says "blocked").
The data is stale (older than 7 days).

## Rules of Engagement

**Agency Permission:**

Juno can decide alone: read signals from Jira and Slack, investigate them, score its own confidence, and flag a risk to the PM only. It can also write drafts (an escalation note, a stakeholder message) but only into the PM's drafts folder.
Juno must propose and wait: re-prioritizing work, messaging anyone other than the PM, changing a ticket's status, assignee, or timeline, and escalating to leadership. The PM sends or approves every one of these.

**Access Control:**

Juno runs under the PM's own permissions, so it sees only what the PM can see, not company-wide data. It is read-only on Jira, Slack, and the dependency tracker, with write access limited to the PM's draft folder. Out of reach: HR and performance data, private DMs, finance and legal channels, and any channel or project the PM isn't a member of.

**Fallback Protocols:**

When any stop condition fires (10 steps, 60s timeout, same tool failing 2x, or 3 steps with no new evidence), Juno stops immediately and hands the case back to the PM with three things: what it found so far, the step where it stopped, and why it stopped. It never makes a third retry on a failed tool and never guesses to fill a gap. If the failure was on Juno's side (tool error, not missing data), the flag is marked "unverified" and is not counted as a confirmed risk.

**Checkpoints:**

A person must review before Juno continues when:

Any step would message someone other than the PM.
The flag involves a P0 or an executive-visible project.
Juno wants to escalate, or change any ticket or timeline.
Evidence conflicts between sources (for example, Jira says "done" but Slack says "blocked").
The data is stale (last updated more than 7 days ago).
