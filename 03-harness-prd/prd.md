## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno's Role Juno is an autonomous AI agent designed to assist product teams by synthesizing signals from various sources, drafting specifications, and prioritizing delivery risks. Its role is to act as a proactive orchestrator, leveraging its environment and capabilities to support informed decision-making and efficient workflows within RocketShip's product ecosystem.  Juno's Task Juno's primary task is to analyze contextual data, perform targeted actions using available tools, and provide clear, verified insights to facilitate rapid product development and operational reliability. It continuously monitors environments, fetches relevant data, executes predefined operations, and validates outcomes—all within specified permissions and retry logic—ultimately contributing to the company's strategic pillars of trust, speed, and scalability.. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

Access to internal monitoring dashboards and logs.
Read-only access to the CRM and sales pipeline data.
Environmental signals: system status, recent alerts, and incident reports.
External integrations like Slack, Jira, or in-house APIs for retrieving incident or ticket statuses.
Restrictions: Only read access to production data, no capability to modify or delete data source configurations.

**How fresh must it be**

Triggered automatically on code deployment or environment change.
Update at every run, or manually refreshed during iteration.

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

deploy_prototype(url) — Deploy or update prototype at URL.
configure_harness(settings) — Set harness parameters for prototype.
run_tests() — Execute tests against prototype endpoint.
collect_logs() — Gather logs and performance metrics.
notify_team(message) — Send status updates.
route_to_human(reason) — Escalate issues to human review.

**Deliberate omissions**

No direct monetary transactions (charge_guest()).
No user messaging (message_guest()).
No irreversible actions without human confirmation.

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

Max retries per operation: 3.
Backoff: exponential starting at 2 seconds, doubling each retry.
Total maximum retry duration: 10 minutes.
Escalate after 2 consecutive failures or conflicting outputs.

**Latency and cost target**

Target: under 12 seconds per cycle.
Cost: approximately $0.10 per process, aligned to operational budget.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

Log artifacts, summaries, and audits stored securely in cloud.
Store last successful execution timestamp, configuration state.
Temporary data cleared unless explicitly marked for retention.
Data about decision rationale stored for audit.

**Expiry and write rules**

Human override decisions are final and stored permanently.
System decisions stored with timestamps for traceability.
Data retention: 90 days unless flagged otherwise.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | blocked |

**Justification**

Read: Environment signals, logs include minimal token waste.
Draft: Unfinished determinations that do not impact other systems.
Write: Confirmed decisions impacting stakeholders or systems.
Send: Blocked to prevent automated irreversible actions.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

Decision cites at least one verbatim environment or system log.
Cross-checks signals and logs for verification.
Validates file hashes or schemas for correctness.
Uses external monitoring systems for confirmation.
If verification fails, stops and escalates to human review.

**Failure behaviour**

Halt process with a report: “I do not have enough evidence to decide”.
Routes to a human operator for review and decision.
Never produces unverified or paraphrased citations.
